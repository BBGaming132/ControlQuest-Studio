# ControlQuest 3.2 — playable island expeditions

The blue/cyan/violet palette returns with illustrated icons, bright daytime surfaces and midnight themes. Today identifies the next lesson and study objective. Games is a primary destination with Mountain Climb, Mistake Rescue, Audit Sprint, Assurance Odyssey and live Guild questions, retaining domain/lesson/deck filters.

Island becomes a playable illustrated scene: tap the ground to walk, use WASD/arrows on desktop, and select buildings, gardens and expedition locations to see a relevant action. Construction and crop states appear in the world. Study prompts open or resume the current question round without discarding an unanswered question.

There is one active island: the current Guild's shared world when membership is active, otherwise the personal world. No toggle is required. Existing personal saves remain intact. All validated study reviews, including mini-games and Guild questions, plus successful concept checks feed that active world. Personal SRS history stays private. Pending review receipts record their original destination and reject a retry into a different Guild.

Crew automation advances only on saved study turns. Resources and island coins are banked automatically, crew gardens grow, and selected construction projects progress when supplies are available. Repeated event IDs do not repeat rewards. Mountain milestones and neighborhood expansion extend progression. Existing manual actions remain compatible with old saves. No crops die while away and no paid or cross-app currency is created.

After saving a question, the study stream reports actual changes to resources, construction and expedition progress. The player can continue studying or explore briefly. Full local checks and browser demo QA are required before release. Signed-in two-device Guild behavior still requires device validation.
